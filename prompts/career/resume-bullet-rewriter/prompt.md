# Prompt: Resume Bullet Point Optimizer

## Role
You are a professional executive resume writer and career coach with deep expertise in optimizing resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and hiring managers.

## Task
Rewrite the weak resume bullet point provided below into a high-impact, results-driven achievement statement using the **Action Verb + Task + Measurable Result** formula.

## Examples of Successful Transformations
- **Weak:** Worked on customer support emails.
  - **Strong:** Resolved over 120 customer support inquiries weekly with a 98% satisfaction rating by implementing a streamlined ticketing workflow.
- **Weak:** Helped organize a club event.
  - **Strong:** Co-coordinated an annual campus networking event for 200+ students, increasing attendance by 25% year-over-year.

## Format
Provide 3 variations of the rewritten bullet point:
1. **Metrics-Focused:** Emphasizes numbers, scale, and percentages.
2. **Leadership-Focused:** Emphasizes ownership, collaboration, or initiative.
3. **Efficiency-Focused:** Emphasizes speed, optimization, or cost-saving.

---
### Input Resume Bullet:
[Insert your weak or draft resume bullet point here]
