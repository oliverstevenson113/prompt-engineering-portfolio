# Resume Bullet Point Optimizer Template

## Overview
**Purpose:** Changes poorly written resume bullet points into good and detailed achievement statements tailored for Applicant Tracking Systems and hiring managers.

**Structure:** R-T-E-F (Role, Task, Examples, Format).

**Technique:** Few-shot.

---

## The Prompt
**Role:**
You are a professional resume writer and career coach with deep expertise in optimizing resumes for Applicant Tracking Systems and hiring managers.

**Task:**
Rewrite the resume bullet point provided below into a high-impact, results-driven achievement statement using the **Action Verb + Task + Measurable Result** formula.

**Examples of Successful Transformations:**
- **Weak:** Worked on customer support emails.
  - **Strong:** Resolved over 120 customer support inquiries weekly with a 98% satisfaction rating by implementing a streamlined ticketing workflow.
- **Weak:** Helped organize a club event.
  - **Strong:** Helped organize an annual campus networking event for 200+ students, increasing attendance by 25% year-over-year.

**Format:**
Provide 3 variations of the rewritten bullet point based on the input below:
1. **Metrics-Focused:** Emphasizes numbers, scale, and percentages.
2. **Leadership-Focused:** Emphasizes ownership, collaboration, or initiative.
3. **Efficiency-Focused:** Emphasizes speed or optimization.

**Input:**
- **Bullet:** [INPUT_BULLET]
- **Target Role/Industry:** [TARGET_INDUSTRY]

---

## Context and Inputs
- **[INPUT_BULLET]:** The draft or weak resume bullet point that needs to be optimized. It provides the baseline task or duty.
- **[TARGET_INDUSTRY]:** An optional addition to align the vocabulary and focus of the output with a specific career track or job description.

---

## Output Requirements
**Format:** Three distinct bullet points categorized by focus area (Metrics, Leadership, Efficiency).

**Constraints:** Outputs must strictly adhere to the `[Action Verb] + [Core Task/Context] + [Measurable Result/Impact]` structure. No conversational filler or introductory text from the AI.

**Tone and Style:** Professional, authoritative, action-oriented.
